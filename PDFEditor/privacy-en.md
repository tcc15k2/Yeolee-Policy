# PDF Editor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09
Operator: Yeolee · Contact: tcc15k2@gmail.com
Korean version: https://tcc15k2.github.io/Yeolee-Policy/PDFEditor/privacy.html

## 1. What PDF Editor does
PDF Editor reads and edits PDFs. You can add pen, highlighter, text boxes, photos and signatures to a PDF, reorder pages, and merge or split files. It can also scan paper documents, turn photos into a PDF, and open Hangul (HWP, HWPX), Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents. There is no sign-up, and the operator runs no server of its own.

## 2. Your documents stay on your device
Documents, scanned images and signatures are processed **only on your device**. Their contents, file names and signature images are not sent to the operator or to advertising or analytics companies. The screen that draws documents runs with internet requests blocked.

The app has internet permission for ads, usage statistics and purchases. That traffic is separate from document processing and involves only the information in section 4.

## 3. Information stored only on your device
| Information | Contents | Purpose | How it is deleted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recent files | File name, file location reference, size, format, last opened time, favorite | Reopening documents | From the list or settings in the app, or by uninstalling |
| My documents | PDFs you create or edit and save (in your device's Documents › PDFEditor folder) and their document type (receipt, contract, report, other) | Managing documents | Delete in the app or in a file manager |
| Saved signatures | Signature images you draw and save | Adding signatures to documents | Delete on the signature screen, or by uninstalling |
| App settings | Theme, document color inversion, Pro purchase state, whether the footer line is on, a save counter used to time the review request | Keeping your settings | By uninstalling |
| Temporary files | Short-lived files in the app's private storage while scanning, converting photos or editing | Work before saving | When you save or close the screen, and automatically within one hour |

This information is never sent off your device and is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Document passwords are never stored.

PDFs saved with the free version get one line, "Made with PDF Editor", at the bottom of the last page. You can turn it off on the save screen, and Pro users never get it. The line contains no personal information.

Google processes information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Ad consent and your choices
In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice under "Privacy options" in the app settings. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.

**US state privacy rights.** Some US states, such as California, treat certain advertising data as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. For interest-based ads, the app lets Google and its advertising partners receive identifiers such as the advertising ID, usage such as app launches and taps, and approximate location inferred from the IP address. The operator receives no direct payment for this data, and it never includes document contents, file names or signatures. To opt out, choose "Don't sell or share my data" under "Privacy options" in the app settings; the option appears in those states. Ads may still appear, but not based on this data, and the app works the same. You may also have rights to access, correct and delete personal information. The operator keeps no such information; for help, write to the address in section 9 or use your Google settings at https://myadcenter.google.com.

## 6. Permissions
| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Internet, network state | Ads, usage statistics, purchases |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Ads |
| Write storage (Android 9 only) | Saving created PDFs to the Documents › PDFEditor folder |

The app does not request camera permission; scanning is handled by the Google Play services scanner screen. Photos are read only when you pick them in the system photo picker. The app reads only documents you choose or that another app hands to it.

## 7. Retention and deletion
The operator does not collect or keep personal information. Information stored on your device disappears when you uninstall the app, except PDFs saved to your Documents › PDFEditor folder, which are your files and stay. Third-party retention follows each provider's policy and settings.

## 8. Children
The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect children's information.
