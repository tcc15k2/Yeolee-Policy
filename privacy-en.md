# CameraScanner Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11
Operator: Yeolee
Contact: tcc15k2@gmail.com

## 1. Summary

- Photos you scan, PDFs you create and recognized text are **processed and stored only on your device**. They are never sent to a server.
- There is no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email address.
- To show ads and improve the app, **an advertising ID, usage statistics and crash information** are processed by Google services (AdMob, Firebase).
- You can turn usage statistics and crash reporting off at any time in Settings.

## 2. Information processed only on your device

| Information | How it is handled |
|---|---|
| Scanned images, imported PDFs and photos | Stored in app-private storage. Not uploaded. |
| Text recognition (OCR) results | Recognized on-device and stored in an on-device database. |
| Signature images, folder and document names | Stored only on your device. |

Documents are not included in backups. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## 3. Information processed by external services

| Service | Purpose | Information |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Showing ads | Advertising ID, device and app information, ad interactions, approximate location (IP-based) |
| Google User Messaging Platform | Ad consent | Your consent choice |
| Firebase Analytics | Usage statistics | App instance ID; events such as screen views, saves and exports (no names or content) |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Stability | Crash logs, device model, OS version |
| Firebase Remote Config | Feature and ad settings | The device downloads settings; no personal data is sent |
| Google ML Kit | Document scanning, QR detection | Processed on-device; Google Play services downloads the module |

These services follow Google's privacy policies:
- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 4. Your choices

- **Usage statistics and crash reporting:** Settings → "Usage statistics and crash reports".
- **Ad personalization:** Device Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.
- **Ad consent:** In regions where consent is required, a consent form appears before the first ad.
- **Deleting data:** Uninstall the app to delete all documents. We hold no personal data on a server.

## 5. Retention

- On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Firebase analytics data: event data is kept for 2 months and then deleted.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## 7. Changes

We will announce changes in the app or on the store page.
