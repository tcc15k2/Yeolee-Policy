## 1. What FastCad does
FastCad (FastCad PDF Viewer) opens DWG, DXF, PDF and image drawings so you can view them, measure lengths, areas and angles, add markup, and export to PDF or images. There is no sign-up or sign-in.

## 2. Information stored only on your phone
Drawings are read and converted **only on your phone** and are never sent to a server. The information below is also stored only on your phone.

| Information | Contents | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Copies of opened drawings | A copy of each file you open (in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read) | Reopening a drawing even if the original was moved or deleted |
| Recent files | File name, format, size, time last opened | Recent files on the home screen |
| Measurements, markup, calibration | Measurements, markup, scale calibration and layer visibility per drawing | Restoring them when you reopen a drawing |
| Conversion cache | Converted drawing data kept so files reopen quickly | Opening speed |
| Settings and usage state | Units, display settings, language, number of drawings opened during the free Pro trial, expiry time of a pass earned by watching an ad | App behavior |

- The app only accesses files you pick yourself (or pass to it with "Open with" from another app). It does not ask for access to all of your storage.
- Exported PDFs and images go only where you choose (the app you share to). The share text includes this app's Google Play link.

## 3. External services

| Service | Information sent | Purpose | How to turn it off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (may be used to estimate approximate location), interactions such as app launches and taps, app and ads SDK performance diagnostics | Showing banner ads and rewarded ads (watch an ad to get a Pro feature pass), measuring ad performance, preventing fraud | No ads after buying Pro. Settings > Privacy options (in regions where consent is required) |
| Google Play billing | Purchase information (handled by Google Play; the app never receives card details) | Confirming the Pro purchase | — |
| Google Play in-app updates | (Talks to Google Play directly) installed version | Telling you about new versions | — |

- Ads appear only as a banner on the home screen and as rewarded ads you choose to watch. They never appear in the drawing viewer.
- In regions where consent is required, consent is collected with Google's consent management (UMP) form, which you can reopen in Settings.
- Information sent by AdMob goes to Google and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). All transfers are encrypted with TLS. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.
- Drawing contents, file names and measurements are never sent to any service.

## 4. Retention and deletion
- Removing a file from the recent files list deletes its copy and conversion cache. You can choose whether its measurements and markup are deleted too.
- Settings > Clear cache deletes the conversion cache.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone.
- The operator keeps no user information on a server. Questions: tcc15k2@gmail.com.

## 5. Children
The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect children's information.
