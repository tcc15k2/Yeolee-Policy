## 1. The service
SnapSolve shows an AI-made answer and solution based on a photo of a math problem that you take or choose.

## 2. Age
This app is for people 14 or older. The app does not store the birth year and month you enter; it records on your phone only whether you can use the app.

## 3. AI solutions
- Solutions are made by an AI model (Anthropic Claude) and may be wrong. For anything important, check again with a teacher or a textbook.
- To make the solution, the photo is sent through the operator's server to the AI provider (Anthropic, United States) and is not stored on the operator's server. Please make sure the photo shows no personal information or other people's faces.
- Photos that are not math problems, or that cannot be read, may not be solved.

## 4. Ads and subscriptions
- Free solves may be given after you watch an ad.
- With a subscription, you can solve without ads up to a set daily limit. Payment, cancellation and refunds for subscriptions follow Google Play's policies and are managed in Google Play.

## 5. Restrictions
Use that calls the server automatically, modifies the app, or disrupts the service may be restricted.

## 6. Reports
You can report a wrong or inappropriate solution in the app. When you report, the solution text and the report reason are kept on the server for 90 days. Photos are not kept.

## 7. Data deletion
You can delete your data in the app's settings. Details follow the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## 8. Changes and contact
If these terms change, we will announce it on this page and in the app. Send questions to Yeolee · tcc15k2@gmail.com.
