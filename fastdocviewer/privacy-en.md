# FastDocViewer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05
Operator: Yeolee · Contact: tcc15k2@gmail.com
Korean version: https://tcc15k2.github.io/Yeolee-Policy/fastdocviewer/privacy.html

## 1. What FastDocViewer does
FastDocViewer is a read-only viewer for Hangul (HWP, HWPX), PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents. There is no sign-up, and the operator runs no server of its own.

## 2. Your documents stay on your device
Documents are processed **only on your device**. Their contents and file names are not sent to the operator or to advertising or analytics companies. The screen that draws documents runs with internet requests blocked.

The app has internet permission for ads and usage statistics. That traffic is separate from document processing and involves only the information in section 4.

## 3. Information stored only on your device
| Information | Contents | Purpose | How it is deleted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recent files | File name, file location reference, size, format, last opened time, favorite | Reopening documents | From the list or settings in the app, or by uninstalling |
| App settings | Theme, document color inversion, ad removal purchase state | Keeping your settings | By uninstalling |
| Temporary copy | A short-lived copy in the app's private storage, made when another app hands over a file in a way that needs it | Showing the document | Automatically when the document closes or the app restarts |

This information is not sent off your device and is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Document passwords are not stored.

## 4. Third-party services
| Service | Provider | Information processed | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Google | IP address, device and account identifiers such as the advertising ID, usage such as app launches and taps, diagnostics | Showing ads, ad analytics, fraud prevention |
| Google Analytics for Firebase | Google | App instance ID, usage events (document format, file size range, open success or failure, and similar), device information | Understanding stability and usage |
| Google Play Billing | Google | Ad removal purchase information | Payment and restoring purchases |
| Google Play app updates | Google | App version information | Offering updates |

Usage events never include file names, paths or document contents. After you buy ad removal, the app does not request ads. In regions that require consent, analytics data is sent only with your consent. If you opt out under section 5 in a US state, analytics data is not sent either.

Google handles this information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Ad consent and your choices
In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice under "Privacy options" in the app's settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

**US state privacy rights.** Some US states, including California, treat certain ad data as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. For interest-based ads, the app lets Google and its ad partners receive identifiers such as the advertising ID, usage such as app launches and taps, and approximate location estimated from the IP address. The operator receives no money for this data directly and never includes document contents or file names. To opt out, choose "Don't sell or share my data" under "Privacy options" in the app's settings; that option appears in the states it applies to. You may then still see ads, but they will not be based on this data, and the app works the same either way. Those states may also give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information. The operator holds none of it, so contact us at the address in section 9 for help, or use Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.

## 6. Permissions
| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Internet | Ads, usage statistics |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Ads |

The app does not request storage permission. It reads only the documents you pick or that another app hands to it.

## 7. Retention and deletion
The operator does not directly collect or keep personal information. Information stored on your device disappears when you uninstall the app. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies and settings.

## 8. Children
The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information.
