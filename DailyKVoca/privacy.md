# Daily K-Voca Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-15
Operator: Yeolee · Contact: tcc15k2@gmail.com

## 1. What the app does
Daily K-Voca is an app for learning Korean words and Hangul. No account or sign-up is needed.

## 2. Data stored only on your phone
| Data | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Study records | Review stage and due date per word, daily study counts and correct answers | Review schedule and stats |
| Favorites and collection | Saved words, time first completed | Favorites and collection screens |
| Study profile | Start level, daily goal, chosen word list, display options | Build your daily words |
| Games and Hangul progress | Today's word list and progress, game high scores, missed words, lesson progress | Resume and game questions |
| App settings | Voice setting, reminder settings, ad frequency record | Keep the app working |

## 3. Microphone and voice
- The app uses the microphone only while you turn on voice commands (for example "next", "repeat"). If you deny the permission, everything else still works.
- Voice is processed by your phone's **on-device speech recognizer**. The app does not save voice and does not send it to a server.
- Usage statistics record only the command type. Voice and recognized text are not recorded.
- Korean audio is played with your phone's text-to-speech voice. No text is sent by the app for this.

## 4. External services
| Service | Data passed | Purpose | How to stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID, IP address (may give approximate location), ad views and clicks, device information | Show and measure ads, prevent fraud | Reset or delete the advertising ID in phone settings. Premium removes ads. |
| Firebase Authentication (Google) | A user ID (an anonymous ID the app creates). No name, email or phone number | Link your review records to the anonymous ID | — |
| Cloud Firestore (Google) | Anonymous ID and per-word review stage, interval, due time, review and miss counts, time first completed | Back up review records | Settings > Delete cloud study records |
| Firebase Analytics (Google) | Study and game start and end, scores, voice command type, streak card views and shares, review prompt shown, device information, app instance ID | Improve the app | — |
| Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) | Push token | Deliver notifications | Turn off notifications |
| Google Play Billing | Purchase information (handled by Google Play; the app never receives card details) | Verify your subscription | Cancel in Google Play |

- Ads are not shown during study sessions.
- Where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), the app asks through Google's consent form (UMP).
- Data passed to Google is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- The operator does not sell personal data.
- Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers.

## 5. Retention and deletion
- Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your phone.
- Cloud review records are stored only under the anonymous ID and are not deleted automatically when you uninstall.
- Open **Settings > Delete cloud study records** to delete the server records and the anonymous ID right away. Records on your phone stay, and later study is backed up under a new anonymous ID. Please do this before uninstalling.
- If you cannot use the app, email tcc15k2@gmail.com and ask for deletion. The operator deletes the records it can identify without undue delay.
- Study records and settings on your phone may be included in your Android/Google backup if device backup is on.

## 6. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, email the address above.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. Use the in-app deletion or email the address above.

## 8. Changes
If this policy changes, this page is updated with a new effective date.
