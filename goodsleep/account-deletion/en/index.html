GoodSleep: Account and data deletion request

GoodSleep (developed by Yeolee) works without signing in. Data is stored on a server only if you signed in with a Google account for backup, and you can delete it in the ways below.

Option 1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open GoodSleep and go to Profile → Account and backup.
  2. Tap Delete account and backups and confirm.
  3. Your server backup and sign-in account are deleted. (Records on this phone remain. To remove them too, use Profile → Delete data.)

Option 2. Request by email if you cannot use the app

Email tcc15k2@gmail.com with the following.

  • Subject: GoodSleep account deletion request
  • Body: the email address of the Google account you signed in with

To verify it is you, we reply to that account's email address. We delete the data within 7 days of verification and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Sleep records (including 30-second sleep stages), mood and notes, saved mixes, sleep goals, settings backup
  • Sign-in account information (email, name, identifier)

Sound clips are never uploaded and exist only on your phone. We hold no information that we are required by law to keep.

Privacy policy: https://tcc15k2.github.io/Yeolee-Policy/goodsleep/privacy-en.html
